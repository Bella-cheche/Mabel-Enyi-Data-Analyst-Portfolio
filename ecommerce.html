KEY INSIGHTS

What the analysis revealed

The analysis revealed important patterns across customers, products and transactions while also highlighting data quality considerations that should be accounted for when interpreting performance.

01

Transaction volume is concentrated

A relatively small group of markets and customers contributes a significant share of transaction activity, creating opportunities for targeted commercial strategies.

02

Cancelled transactions matter

Cancelled orders form an important part of the dataset and should be separated from completed sales when evaluating actual business performance.

03

Missing customer information requires attention

A substantial number of records contain missing customer identifiers. These records should be handled carefully when measuring customer-level performance.

04

Zero-price records require investigation

Zero-price transactions appear strongly associated with cancelled orders in the dataset, making them important when validating sales calculations.

05

Customer segmentation improves visibility

Grouping customers based on transaction behavior creates a clearer basis for understanding purchasing patterns and supporting targeted engagement.

06

Data quality affects decision-making

Cleaning and validating transaction records is essential because missing identifiers, cancellations and unusual values can influence reported business performance.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning insights into action

01. Focus on high-value markets and customers

Use customer and geographic transaction patterns to prioritize retention, engagement and sales strategies.

02. Monitor cancelled transactions

Track cancellation patterns separately from completed sales to identify potential operational or customer experience issues.

03. Improve customer data capture

Strengthen customer information collection to improve customer-level reporting and future segmentation.

04. Maintain data quality checks

Establish routine validation for missing values, zero-price records and cancelled transactions before business reporting.

CONCLUSION

Better data, clearer commercial decisions

This project demonstrates how transaction-level e-commerce data can be transformed into meaningful business insight. By combining SQL analysis, data preparation and Power BI visualization, the analysis provides a clearer understanding of sales activity, customer behavior and transaction quality.
