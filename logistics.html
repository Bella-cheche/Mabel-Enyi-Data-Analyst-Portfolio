PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding Logistics Performance Through Data

This project analyzes logistics operations across revenue, operating costs, trips, fleet utilization, driver performance, safety, and profitability. The goal was to transform operational data into clear insights that can support better decision-making across the business.

End-to-End Analysis covering operations, finance and safety
BUSINESS PROBLEM

What was the analysis designed to understand?

Logistics operations generate large volumes of operational and financial data. Without a unified analytical view, it can be difficult to understand what is driving revenue, where costs are concentrated, how efficiently the fleet is being utilized, and where operational risks exist.

Operational Performance

How efficiently are trips, vehicles and operational resources being utilized?

Driver Performance

What patterns can be identified across driver activity, performance and operational outcomes?

Financial Performance

How do revenue, fuel, maintenance and other costs affect overall profitability?

Safety & Risk

Where are safety-related and financial risk patterns visible in the operational data?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

From operational data to actionable insights

The analysis combined multiple operational tables into a structured analytical model and created measures to evaluate revenue, operating costs, profitability and operational performance.

01

Data Preparation

Reviewed and prepared the source data for analysis, ensuring the required fields were structured consistently.

02

Data Modeling

Organized the 14-table dataset into a structured analytical model for reporting and analysis.

03

Metric Development

Developed measures for revenue, operating costs, net profit, profit margin and trip performance.

04

Dashboard Analysis

Built interactive Power BI dashboards to examine operational, driver, safety and financial performance.

KEY INSIGHTS

What the analysis revealed

The dashboards provide a connected view of operational performance and financial outcomes, allowing the business to examine profitability alongside the operational factors influencing it.

01

Profitability improved

Profit margin increased from 58.89% to 64.74%, indicating a stronger profitability position across the analyzed operations.

02

Revenue and cost performance are connected

Examining revenue alongside fuel and maintenance costs provides a clearer understanding of the factors affecting net profit.

03

Fleet performance requires continuous monitoring

Fleet utilization and trip activity provide important operational indicators for identifying underused or inefficient resources.

04

Safety is also a business consideration

Monitoring safety indicators alongside financial measures helps connect operational risk with potential business impact.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning insights into action

01. Monitor fleet utilization

Track vehicle and trip utilization regularly to identify underused resources and improve operational efficiency.

02. Manage major operating costs

Monitor fuel and maintenance costs closely and investigate significant changes that could affect profitability.

03. Use driver performance data

Incorporate driver-level performance indicators into regular operational reviews to identify improvement opportunities.

04. Strengthen safety monitoring

Maintain consistent visibility into safety indicators so potential operational risks can be investigated early.

CONCLUSION

A connected view of logistics performance

This project demonstrates how operational, financial, fleet, driver and safety data can be brought together into a single analytical framework. By connecting these areas, the analysis provides a clearer picture of how operational activity relates to revenue, costs and profitability.
