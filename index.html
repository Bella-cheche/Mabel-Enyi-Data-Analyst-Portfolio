ABOUT ME

I look beyond the numbers to understand the story they tell.

I work with data to understand what is happening, uncover the patterns behind it, and communicate those findings in a way that supports informed business decisions. My analytical work spans SQL, Power BI, and Excel, from cleaning and validating datasets to exploring customer behaviour, operational performance, and sales trends. I turn these findings into clear, interactive dashboards and meaningful reports that make complex information easier to understand. I enjoy the part of analysis where numbers become a story — identifying the patterns that matter, asking what may be driving them, and translating the evidence into clear insights and practical business recommendations.
